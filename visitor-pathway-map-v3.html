How the three pathways connect
Markets pages cross-link to relevant Capabilities and Locations. EMS buyers can also browse Capabilities from the homepage if they choose.
Capabilities pages cross-link to relevant Markets and Locations. OSAT buyers and NPI engineers often land directly on a sub-pillar page from search — they're confirming Hana has the specific process they need before making contact. This is Hana's core strength: breadth of capability across EMS and OSAT.
Locations pages cross-link to Markets and Capabilities. Location-specific buyers land directly from "capability + country" searches. RFQ and Contact available at facility page and capability section level.
